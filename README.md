# SPLICETRACKER

## Smart Multi-Layer Conveyor Belt Splice & Condition Monitoring System

SPLICETRACKER is a multi-layer conveyor belt inspection and condition-monitoring system designed to detect abnormal belt conditions, validate sensor observations, localize defects, track degradation over time, and provide actionable maintenance information.

The system combines embedded sensing, data validation, sensor fusion, condition assessment, temporal tracking, and a live monitoring dashboard into a unified inspection architecture.

---

## Problem

Conveyor-belt splices and surrounding belt sections are vulnerable to mechanical and environmental degradation.

Current inspection practices may depend on:

- Periodic manual inspection
- Limited inspection coverage
- Single-sensor observations
- Delayed identification of developing defects
- Manual interpretation of large amounts of belt-condition information

Defects that develop between inspection intervals can remain unnoticed until they become more severe, potentially resulting in:

- Conveyor downtime
- Increased maintenance requirements
- Material and operational losses
- Safety risks

SPLICETRACKER addresses this by combining multiple sensing methods and correlating their observations instead of relying on a single source of information.
